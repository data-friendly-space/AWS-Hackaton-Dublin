Powered by GOAL Global

Resilio is built on GOAL's Resilience for Social Systems (R4S) methodology, developed through decades of humanitarian response experience across 14 countries worldwide.

Our Mission

Resilio empowers humanitarian organizations to understand, map, and strengthen the social systems that protect vulnerable communities. Through AI-assisted analysis, we transform months of complex research into actionable insights, enabling faster, more effective resilience-building that helps communities not just survive crises, but thrive beyond them.

Brand Essence

The core principles that define Resilio

Resilience

Our name comes from the Latin root meaning "to spring back." We help systems bend without breaking and recover stronger.

Connection

Social systems are networks of relationships. We reveal the hidden connections that make communities strong or vulnerable.

Clarity

We transform complex data into clear insights. Understanding precedes action, and we make the complex comprehensible.

Impact

Every analysis serves a purpose: protecting the most vulnerable by strengthening the systems they depend on.

Logo Symbolism

The Resilio mark represents interconnected actors within a social system. The outer circle symbolizes the system boundary, while the three connected nodes represent different actor types (service providers, regulators, and support actors). The central highlighted node represents the service user at the heart of every system.

Do

  • Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "R"
  • Use approved color combinations only
  • Scale proportionally
  • Use on clean, uncluttered backgrounds

Don't

  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Add effects like shadows or gradients
  • Place on busy backgrounds
  • Change the logo colors

Voice & Tone

How Resilio communicates with users and stakeholders

Clear

We translate complex systems analysis into understandable insights. Avoid jargon; use plain language.

Purposeful

Every word serves the mission. Focus on actionable information that helps protect communities.

Respectful

We honor the dignity of vulnerable communities and the expertise of local practitioners.

Empowering

We provide tools and insights, not prescriptions. Users are the experts in their contexts.

Write

  • "This actor is highly vulnerable to drought conditions"
  • "The relationship between actors A and B is stressed"
  • "Consider strengthening this connection"
  • "3 actors require immediate attention"

Avoid

  • "The stakeholder matrix indicates suboptimal synergies"
  • "Problematic relationship detected!!!"
  • "You must fix this immediately"
  • "Critical failure in system resilience paradigm"
